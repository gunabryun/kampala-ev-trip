# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a welcoming place to improve EV mobility in Uganda and beyond. Participation must be respectful, practical, and safe.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful across experience levels, languages, identities, and backgrounds.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Share evidence and uncertainty honestly.
- Protect privacy and avoid publishing personal, sensitive, or security-relevant information.
- Put rider and public safety before speed, hype, or commercial advantage.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, threats, hate speech, sexualised content, doxxing, stalking, impersonation, spam, knowingly false safety claims, publishing private data, or instructions that enable unsafe vehicle control are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove content, and may restrict participation, when this code is breached. Report concerns privately to **gunabryun007@gmail.com**.
